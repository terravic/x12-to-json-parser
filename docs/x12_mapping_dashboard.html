837 Health Care Claim (Professional 837P & Institutional 837I)

        

          The 837 transaction is utilized by health care providers to submit billing claims and encounter information to payers. The parser organizes flat X12 segments into hierarchical loops: Loop 2010AA (Billing Provider), Loop 2010BA (Subscriber), Loop 2300 (Claim Header), and Loop 2400 (Service Lines).
        

        

          

            Loop 2000A / 2010AA
            
Billing Provider Details (NM1*85, N3, N4, REF*EI). Mapped to billing_provider.

          

          

            Loop 2300 (Claim Header)
            
Claim identifier, total charges (CLM02), dates of service (DTP*472), and diagnosis codes (HI*ABK). Mapped to claims[].

          

          

            Loop 2400 (Service Lines)
            
Line item counter (LX), CPT/HCPCS procedure codes & units (SV1/SV2). Mapped to service_lines[].

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
835 Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA) & Payment Adjudication

        

          The 835 transaction communicates payment explanation, contractual reductions, deductible/coinsurance assignments, and line-item denials from payers to providers.
        

        

          

            Financial Information (BPR)
            
EFT amount, payment method (ACH), routing numbers, and settlement date. Mapped to financial_information.

          

          

            Claim Payment (CLP)
            
Claim status code (1=Paid, 4=Denied), paid amount, and patient responsibility amount. Mapped to claims[].

          

          

            Adjustments (CAS)
            
Group codes (CO/PR/OA) and CARC reason codes (e.g. 45=Exceeds Fee Schedule). Mapped to adjustments[].

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
277 Claim Status Notification & Clinical Attachment Flagging

        

          The 277 transaction provides claim status updates and requests additional information from providers. When a payer pends a claim for clinical review, the parser automatically evaluates STC (Categories R0–R5) and PWK segments to populate a unified required_attachments list.
        

      

    


    
    

      

        
275 Patient Information Attachment & C-CDA XML Integration

        

          The 275 transaction wraps clinical documentation inside standard X12 envelopes. The engine extracts the embedded payload from BDS / BIN segments (handling both plain XML and Base64 encoding) and passes it to the CCDAParser to produce rich clinical objects for Demographics, Medications, Allergies, Problems, Vitals, and Notes.
        

      

    


    
    

      

        
270/271 Eligibility Inquiry & 278 Prior Authorization Review

        

          Parses health plan benefit inquiries and authorization decisions. Maps EB (Eligibility/Benefit Information), EQ (Eligibility Inquiry), UM (Health Care Services Review), and HCR (Review Decision / Approval Action).
